BSDC is a two-day tech conference in Bozeman, Montana, hosted by the folks at Montana Programmers. We bring together developers, engineers, and builders from across the state and beyond for talks that actually matter — hypermedia, AI, systems programming, game dev, and everything in between.

No corporate keynotes. No "thought leadership." Just real people shipping real things, sharing what they've learned, and hanging out in one of the most beautiful places on earth.

We've pulled in over 160,000 video views and 600 attendees since rebooting in 2024. This year we're going bigger — more speakers, lower ticket prices, and the best lineup yet. If you're tired of boring conferences, come build something a little wild with us.

Alexander Petros
Hypermedia is for Governments
Alexander Petros is a software engineer who specializes in making web systems reliable, accessible, and cost-effective. He is the co-author of the Triptych HTML proposals, which would make HTML dramatically more capable with just three small changes. He is currently an Engineering Lead at the IRS. (speaking in his personal capacity)
Christian Tanul
Real-Time Hypermedia (the htmx way)
I created the server-commands extension for htmx, which led to working with Carson on htmx 4.0. Since then I've been full-time on the project, redesigning the site, rewriting docs, and refining the codebase. I self-host all my experiments and pray my hardware doesn't die during live demos (again).
Barrett Breshears
Cognitive Vandalism: What I Learned by Mutilating a GPT
Barrett Breshears is a Staff Engineer at Bridger Aerospace, where he works on mission-critical mobile systems and data-driven applications. He has been building native iOS and cross-platform software for over a decade and has recently focused on local-first AI, large language models, and applied ML systems.
Adam Rackis
Falling in Love with SQL
After a brief stint in the Air Force Adam began his career as a software developer for a small company in Oklahoma City. In the more than decade since he's worked in a wide variety of industries, including Riot Games. He's currently a senior software engineer at Spotify, doing full stack engineering for the Spotify for Artists site.
Stephen Mitchell
A talk
