Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Montana Programmers Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information in connection with Big Sky Dev Con and the website at bigskydevcon.com (the "Event" and "Site"). By registering for the Event or using the Site, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you register for the Event, purchase a ticket, sign up for updates, or contact us. This may include your name, email address, organization, job title, and any other details you choose to share. We also automatically collect certain technical information when you use the Site, such as your IP address, device and browser type, and usage data, including through cookies and similar technologies.

How We Use Information

  • To register attendees, process tickets, and operate the Event.
  • To communicate with you about the Event, including logistics, updates, and related programming.
  • To understand and improve the Site and the attendee experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect the safety and integrity of the Event.

How We Share Information

We may share attendee information with event partners, sponsors, analytics providers, and service providers. We may also share information when required by law, to enforce our terms or community guidelines, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of our organization or assets.

Your Choices

You may opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in those messages, and you can adjust your browser settings to limit cookies. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, contact us using the details below. We will respond consistent with applicable law.

Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this policy and to meet legal and operational requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The Site and Event are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
