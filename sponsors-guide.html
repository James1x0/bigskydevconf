Sponsor & Exhibitor Survival Guide

Big Sky Dev Con 2026 · July 24–25 · Bozeman, MT

TL;DR — Tables are available on both days. You may set up 30 minutes before doors open. Staffing your booth is entirely optional—your space will be reserved and available regardless.

Venues & Dates

BSDC 2026 takes place across two venues over two days. Both venues will have sponsor/exhibitor table space available.

Day 1 — Friday, July 24

The Rialto, Downtown Bozeman

Doors open to attendees at 3:00 PM

Sponsor setup begins at 2:30 PM

Event ends ~6:20 PM

Day 2 — Saturday, July 25

Strand Union Building (SUB), Montana State University

Doors open to attendees at 8:00 AM (light breakfast served)

Sponsor setup begins at 7:30 AM

Event ends ~4:20 PM

Booth Details

  • Tables and chairs will be provided. If you need power, please let us know in advance.
  • Booth space is available on either or both conference days.
  • You are welcome to bring banners, swag, displays, monitors, or demos—whatever helps you connect with attendees.

Staffing Your Booth

Manning your booth is completely optional. We understand you may want to attend talks or simply enjoy the conference. Your reserved space will be set up and available regardless of whether someone is actively staffing it.

If you do plan to staff your booth, two tickets are included at links you have received earlier.

Setup & Teardown

Setup

  • You may arrive 30 minutes prior to doors to set up your space.
  • Day 1: Setup from 2:30 PM. Doors at 3:00 PM.
  • Day 2: Setup from 7:30 AM. Doors at 8:00 AM.
  • A member of our team will be on-site to direct you to your table location.

Teardown

  • Day 1: Please clear your booth by 7:00 PM (40 min after close).
  • Day 2: Please clear your booth by 5:00 PM (40 min after close).
  • Materials cannot be left overnight. Please take everything with you at the end of each day, as the two venues are at different locations.

What We Provide

  • Table and chairs at your booth location
  • Your logo and name on stage screen, video splashes, and the website
  • A special thank-you shoutout on stage during opening remarks

Logistics

  • If you need specific electrical or AV accommodations, reach out ahead of time.
  • Parking (Day 1): Street parking is available in downtown Bozeman around The Rialto.
  • Parking (Day 2): MSU campus parking is available near the Strand Union Building. Saturday parking is generally unrestricted.

Tips for a Great Booth

  • Developers love stickers, and they love useful swag even more.
  • Live demos or interactive displays draw the most traffic.
  • Have a clear call-to-action—whether it's a sign-up form, a QR code, or just good conversation.

Questions?

If you have any questions, need anything, or want to coordinate details, please don't hesitate to reach out.
